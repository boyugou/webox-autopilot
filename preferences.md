# WeBox Preferences (free-form notes)

Anything you want Claude to know about your food preferences that doesn't fit a structured field in `config.yaml`. Free text, any language. Bullet points, paragraphs, lists — whatever.

Claude reads this **every order session** and weighs it as a soft constraint (the structured rules in `config.yaml` are hard).

To update interactively, just say it in chat: *"I don't like sashimi anymore"* or *"Add a note: prefer purple rice when offered"*. Claude will append here.
